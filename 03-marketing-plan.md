# Devorix — Marketing & GTM Plan

> **Decisions and numbers below are subordinate to `MASTER-PLAN.md`.** Rev share is locked at 50/50 (messaging pillar 4 below is updated accordingly); the advertiser pitch should quote the Tier-0 flat-deal range (₹10,000–15,000/mo), not a CPM number — see `MASTER-PLAN.md` §5.2, §6.

**Version 0.1 · Last updated 2026-06-25 · Living document**

## 1. Positioning
**For Indian developers who use AI coding agents**, Devorix turns the "thinking…" wait into rupees — paid instantly over UPI, with the global leaders can't pay India. **For Indian dev-focused brands**, it's the cheapest targeted way to reach developers, billed in INR with a GST invoice.

One-liner: *"Your AI's wait time, your money. Paid in rupees."*

## 2. Two-sided GTM (the category's real bottleneck is the advertiser side)
This is a marketplace: devs (supply of attention) and advertisers (demand). Early on, **advertiser demand is the constraint** — without it, devs earn ~nothing and churn. Run both sides in parallel, but treat advertiser sales as the priority that makes everything else true.

### Side A — Developers (grab the install base)
- **Launch wave:** Show HN, Product Hunt, X/Twitter dev community, r/india + r/developersIndia, Indian dev Discords/Telegram, LinkedIn. Hook: "kickbacks won't pay India — we do." Ride the existing category virality.
- **Content/SEO:** "How Indian developers can earn from AI wait time," comparison vs Kickbacks/IdleAds, UPI payout proof posts.
- **Proof-of-payout marketing:** screenshots of real UPI payouts to early devs = the single most persuasive asset. Manufacture these early even at small scale.
- **Referral loop:** referral bonus / higher temporary rev share for inviting devs (cheap, viral).
- **Campus/community:** Indian engineering colleges, GDSC chapters, hackathons — huge, cheap, fast-growing dev population.

### Side B — Advertisers (make earnings real)
- **ICP:** Indian dev-tool startups, cloud/hosting resellers, EdTech/upskilling (courses, bootcamps), dev job boards/recruiters, DevRel teams, API/SaaS targeting devs.
- **Motion:** Founder-led concierge sales, **fully cold outreach (0 warm intros confirmed)** — target list of 25–30, pitch 12–15 (`MASTER-PLAN.md` §6.3). Offer a flat ₹10,000–15,000/mo pilot, white-glove setup, GST invoice if needed. Case studies → self-serve/CPM later, once 5–10 advertisers (Tier 1).
- **Pitch:** "Be the exclusive sponsor shown to N active Indian developers inside their editor this month, ₹10,000–15,000 flat, full INR billing, measurable clicks." Not a CPM pitch at this scale.

## 3. Funnel & channels summary
- Awareness: launch PR, dev communities, content/SEO, founder X presence.
- Activation (dev): install → sign in → first impression → first ₹ accrued.
- Activation (advertiser): demo → first block bought → campaign live.
- Retention: real payouts (dev), measurable ROI + repeat blocks (advertiser).

## 4. Messaging pillars
1. **India-first / paid in rupees** (vs Kickbacks can't pay India).
2. **Automated + reliable UPI + GST** (vs IdleAds' manual UPI).
3. **Safe & private** — never reads your code; no editor patching; auditable.
4. **Fair split** — 50/50, transparent and consistent (locked 2026-06-28; we compete on payout reliability + GST + speed, not on out-bidding rivals' headline share number).

## 5. Budget posture (early, lean)
Near-zero paid spend at first; win on organic virality + founder distribution + proof-of-payout. Reserve small budget for campus events and one or two dev-newsletter sponsorships. Revisit paid once fill rate and ARPU/dev are proven.

## 6. KPIs
Installs, WAU, activation rate, **fill rate**, # advertisers + repeat rate, ₹ paid out/month (north star), CAC (dev ≈ ~0 organic; advertiser via founder time), payback.

## 7. 30/60/90 (marketing)
- **0–30:** Build launch assets + proof-of-payout pipeline; line up 5–10 advertiser conversations; viral launch into Indian dev communities.
- **30–60:** Publish first real UPI payout proofs; close first paying advertisers; referral loop live; comparison content ranking.
- **60–90:** Case studies from advertisers; campus/community push; begin self-serve advertiser funnel; explore enterprise/DevRel B2B conversations.

## 8. Assets from the AI Studio prototype
- **Wait-State Coach as a lead-gen tool:** a public "how much could you earn?" estimator (IDE + languages + queries/day → estimate + sponsor matches) is a strong top-of-funnel hook and waitlist driver. Ship it as a marketing page.
- **Ad-Copy Generator for advertisers:** lets cold-outreach advertisers self-generate tasteful sponsorship lines in seconds — lowers friction in the concierge sale.
- **⚠️ Overpromising guardrail:** the prototype shows ₹0.42/impression (~$8–14 eCPM), far above our conservative base and observed reality. **Marketing must show earnings as ranges with conservative defaults, clearly labeled "estimates."** Manufacture credibility with real UPI-payout screenshots, not inflated projections — overpromising is the fastest way to churn devs and burn reputation in a skeptical dev audience.
