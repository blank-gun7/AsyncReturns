## 1. Competitive Pricing Reality Check — Kickback at $2.1 CPM

### What $2.1 CPM Actually Means
Kickback runs a **bidding (RTB) system**. $2.1 CPM is their **average clearing price** — meaning:
- The floor is likely $0.5–$1 CPM (low-intent brands bidding for cheap inventory)
- The ceiling has **no cap** — premium brands could be paying $5–8 on Kickback
- **$2.1 is just the weighted average across ALL bids, good and bad**

So the market isn't saying your inventory is worth $2.1. It's saying Kickback's *mixed, unfiltered inventory* averages $2.1.

### Your Positioning vs Kickback

| Factor | Kickback (Bidding) | Async Returns (Fixed CPM) |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $2.1 avg (variable) | $5 fixed (predictable) |
| Inventory quality | Mixed — any dev | Curated — AI-native Indian devs |
| Ad placement | Spinner replacement | Persistent footer (always visible) |
| Billing certainty | Client never knows final cost | Client budgets exactly |
| Fraud risk | Higher (automated bids) | Zero (you verify impressions) |

**Selling point:** *"You know exactly what you pay, every month. No auction surprises. Guaranteed 220,000 impressions for $1,100 flat."*

### Skip Bidding For Now — Here's Why
- RTB infrastructure takes months to build properly
- At 200 devs, you have **no inventory scarcity** — bidding only matters when demand > supply
- Direct sales closes in days; RTB takes weeks of integration and setup
- Revisit bidding at **5,000+ DAUs** when brands will actually compete

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Decision: Fixed CPM, direct sales only.** RTB is a v2 feature, not a blocker.

## 2. Revised Pricing Strategy

> Start at **$5 CPM** — not because the market won't bear more, but because you need **case studies and testimonials more than margin right now.**

| Phase | CPM | Goal | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Launch (now)** | $5 CPM | 2 founding clients, case studies | Month 1–2 |
| **Growth** | $7 CPM | 5–10 active clients | Month 3–5 |
| **Scale** | $10–12 CPM | Premium brands, full inventory | Month 6+ |

Frame $5 as a **"Founding Partner Rate"** — limited to first 5 clients only, non-renewable. Creates urgency and makes clients feel exclusive.

**Negotiation closer if they push back:**
> *"LinkedIn charges you $40 CPM for a dev scrolling at lunch. I charge $5 for one actively waiting for AI to finish their code. The intent gap is not comparable."*

## 3. Per-Impression Economics at $5 CPM

### Per 10-Second Impression

| Party | USD | INR (₹83/$) |
|---|---|---|
| **Client pays** | $0.0050 | ₹0.415 |
| **Your cut (40%)** | $0.0020 | ₹0.166 |
| **Developer earns (60%)** | $0.0030 | ₹0.249 |

### Month 1 Projections — 200 Devs, 50 Prompts/Day

```
200 devs × 50 prompts × 22 working days = 220,000 impressions
220,000 / 1,000 × $5 = $1,100 Gross Revenue
```

| Line Item | USD | INR |
|---|---|---|
| Gross Revenue (billed to client) | $1,100 | ₹91,300 |
| **Your Revenue (40%)** | **$440** | **₹36,520** |
| Total Dev Payouts (60%) | $660 | ₹54,780 |
| Per dev / month (avg) | $3.30 | **₹274** |
| OpEx (infra, Razorpay fees) | ~$80 | ₹6,640 |
| **Net Profit Month 1** | **~$360** | **~₹29,880** |

> [!WARNING]
> At ₹274/dev/month, devs hit the ₹1,000 cashout in ~3.6 months. That's too slow — they'll churn. **Lower the cashout threshold to ₹250–300.** First payout should happen within 3-4 weeks. That's your biggest retention lever.

## 4. Preferred Client Analysis — Who Gives Easy Money & Why

### 🥇 Tier 1 — Easiest, Fastest Closes (Target THIS Week)

#### Indian Developer Tools & VC-Backed SaaS Startups
**Companies:** Razorpay DevTools, Sentry, PostHog, Hasura, DhiWise, Appsmith, Pabbly, 100ms, Evo (Supabase India)

**Why they say YES fast:**
- They already buy developer media (Dev.to, newsletters, podcast sponsorships)
- Indian devs are their **exact ICP** — zero convincing needed
- Marketing budget is VC money — they move fast
- Decision maker = 1 person (founder or Head of Growth)
- They already understand CPM pricing

**How to find them:**
- LinkedIn: Search "Head of Growth" + "Developer tools" + India
- Twitter: Search `hiring devrel` OR `devtools` + India

---

#### Recently Funded Indian Startups (Past 6 Months)
**Why:**
- Burning investor money — their KPI is **spend**, not cost-save
- Marketing teams have monthly budgets they MUST deploy or lose allocation
- They benchmark against LinkedIn ($40 CPM) — your $5 looks like a steal

**How:**
- Crunchbase / YourStory / Inc42 → filter "India" + "SaaS" + "Seed/Series A" + last 6 months
- DM the founder directly on Twitter with a 2-line pitch + site link

---

### 🥈 Tier 2 — Good Budget, Slightly Slower (Month 2)

#### Bootcamps & Upskilling Platforms
**Companies:** Scaler, Coding Ninjas, iNeuron, Newton School, Masai School

**Why:**
- Their entire business model depends on acquiring developers
- Have dedicated performance marketing teams who buy media daily
- Recurring monthly advertiser — low churn on your side once onboarded

---

#### Cloud Providers (India Teams)
**Companies:** AWS India, GCP India, Azure Developer Advocacy

**Why:**
- Massive dev marketing budgets

**How:** Find the Developer Advocate or Field Marketing Manager, not the sales team.

### ❌ Avoid For Now
